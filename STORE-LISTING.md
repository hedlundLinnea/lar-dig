### Svenska

```
Barnens ordbok är en gratis, reklamfri app där barn lär sig sina första ord på svenska och engelska. Stora barnvänliga knappar, animerade bilder, riktiga djurljud och tydligt uttal — perfekt för bekräftade läsbarnsstunder hemma eller i förskolan.

✨ TRE LÄGEN ANPASSADE EFTER ÅLDER
• 1–2 år: Bara stora bilder. Tryck — appen säger ordet och spelar djurljudet.
• 3–4 år: Bilder med en växla-knapp så barnet kan se hur ordet stavas.
• 5–6 år: Quiz-läge! "Hitta lejonet" — barnet lär sig läsa.

📚 6 KATEGORIER, ÖVER 60 ORD
• Djur (lejon, hund, katt, ko, gris, häst, får, elefant, björn …)
• Mat (äpple, banan, mjölk, pizza, ost …)
• Kläder (tröja, byxor, skor, vantar, mössa …)
• Färger (röd, gul, grön, blå …)
• Fordon (bil, buss, tåg, flygplan …)
• Siffror 1–10
```

### Engelska

```
Children's Dictionary is a free, ad-free app where kids learn their first words in Swedish and English. Big, child-friendly buttons, animated pictures, real animal sounds and clear pronunciation — perfect for shared reading time at home or in preschool.

✨ THREE AGE-ADAPTED MODES
• Ages 1–2: Just big pictures. Tap and the app says the word and plays the animal sound.
• Ages 3–4: Pictures with a toggle button so kids can see how the word is spelled.
• Ages 5–6: Quiz mode! "Find the lion" — kids learn to read.

📚 6 CATEGORIES, OVER 60 WORDS
• Animals (lion, dog, cat, cow, pig, horse, sheep, elephant, bear …)
• Food (apple, banana, milk, pizza, cheese …)
• Clothes (shirt, pants, shoes, mittens, cap …)
• Colors (red, yellow, green, blue …)
• Vehicles (car, bus, train, airplane …)
• Numbers 1–10

🔊 REAL ANIMAL SOUNDS
Hear the lion roar, the dog bark and the cat meow. No synthetic sounds — we use real recordings that are free to use (Public Domain from Wikimedia).

🌍 SWEDISH AND ENGLISH
Switch language in settings. All words, sounds and menus are translated. Also pick which voice should read (all voices installed on your phone work).

🔒 100% PRIVACY
• No ads
• No data collection
• No registration
• No subscriptions
• Open source on GitHub (verify yourself)

💚 FREE AND OPEN
The app is completely free. Source code is open (MIT license) — if you're an educator or developer, you can add your own words, sounds or entire categories.

Questions: linneahedlund1@gmail.com
Web: https://barnensordbok.se
```
